\section*{The single assumption and what rests on it}

\subsection*{Assumption (the only non-deductive, non-conventional input of the falsifiable core)}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{(L) Lorentzian realisation of the no-boundary construction.}
There exists a well-defined analytic continuation $\Phi$ of the Euclidean
no-boundary hemisphere $D^4\subset S^4$ to the present Lorentzian patch such
that (i) $\Phi$ is stable against fluctuations, and (ii) $\Phi$ maps the
scale-free Euclidean quotient $\rho_E$ onto the observed
$\Ok/\OL$, and (iii) this identification persists to the present epoch.
\end{quote}

\textbf{Honest status of (L).} \emph{Open and contested.} The Lorentzian
realisation of any no-boundary wavefunction is challenged by the
Feldbrugge--Lehners--Turok instability (PRL 119, 171301; PRD 97, 023509):
the Lorentzian path integral weights tensor fluctuations as growing modes.
CSG does \emph{not} resolve this; its proven stability result is the
\emph{Euclidean} Hessian positivity of the cap saddle, a different object.
The cap saddle is Robin-deformed and distinct from the FLT de~Sitter saddle,
which leaves open---but does not establish---that the deformation evades the
instability. (L) bundles what the manuscript separates as A1 (no-boundary),
A5b (quotient preservation under $\Phi$) and A6 (persistence); they are
coupled manifestations of this one open question, not three independent gaps.

\textbf{Honest qualification: (L) is the only \emph{open} assumption, not the
only assumption.} A self-falsification of the core (verify\_clean\_pipeline.py)
shows the falsifiable claim also rests on two non-vacuous, but \emph{not open},
inputs: (i) \textbf{A3}, that the photon is the sole infrared conformal
contributor, which is literature-supported by the Komargodski--Schwimmer
$a$-theorem but carries three testable sub-assumptions (A3.1 no extra massless
conformal sector beyond SM$+$gravity --- which silently includes the graviton
premise; A3.2 mass-hierarchy genericity; A3.3 CFT applicability of the deep UV
and present IR); and (ii) the \textbf{hemisphere selection} of the $Q$-charge
normalisation, a theorem in value but a choice in relevance, fixed by (L)'s
no-boundary geometry plus the uniqueness criteria (i)--(iv). The precise honest
statement is therefore: \emph{one open assumption (L); plus A3
(literature-supported, with the graviton premise inside A3.1); plus the
hemisphere selection.} ``One assumption'' is a simplification of this.

coincidence. The defensible claim is exactly: \emph{under one open assumption
(L), the photon conformal anomaly yields a parameter-free, sharply falsifiable
